# OpenAI Launches o3-pro: Most Powerful Reasoning Model Yet

**Source:** TechCrunch, April 2026
**URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/openai-o3-pro/

OpenAI has released o3-pro, the most advanced reasoning model in the o-series lineup. The model demonstrates significant improvements in complex mathematical reasoning, scientific analysis, and multi-step problem solving.

## Key Features
- **Extended thinking time**: o3-pro can "think" for up to 10 minutes on complex problems
- **Improved accuracy**: 92% on GPQA Diamond benchmark (up from 87% for o3)
- **Cost**: $200/month as part of ChatGPT Pro tier
- **API pricing**: $20/1M input tokens, $80/1M output tokens

## Industry Impact
- Direct competitor to Anthropic's Claude 3.5 Opus reasoning capabilities
- Google DeepMind's Gemini 2.5 Pro also targets this segment
- The "reasoning model" category is now the primary battleground for AI labs

## Notable Limitations
- Slower response times compared to standard models (30-60 seconds typical)
- Higher cost may limit adoption for routine tasks
- Still struggles with some creative writing tasks where speed matters
