AI safety company behind Claude. Operates Claude.ai, Claude Code, and the Claude Skills ecosystem. Founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers.

## Key Products

- **Claude:** Flaghship LLM assistant
- **Claude Code:** Command-line coding agent with plugin/skill support
- **Skills Platform:** Extensible prompt framework for enterprise and individual use
