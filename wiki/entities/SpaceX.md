# SpaceX

## Overview

SpaceX is evolving from a **launch provider** to an **AI infrastructure company**. With Starlink (global connectivity), Starship (heavy lift), Starshield (defense/AI), and existing partnerships with Anthropic, xAI, and now Google, SpaceX is positioned as a central player in the next phase of AI infrastructure.

## Key Capabilities Relevant to AI

| Asset | AI Infrastructure Role |
|-------|------------------------|
| **Starlink** | Global low-latency connectivity layer |
| **Starship** | Heavy lift capacity for large-scale orbital infrastructure |
| **Starshield** | Defense and AI compute integration |
| **GPU/Energy footprint** | AI compute capacity (evidenced by Anthropic deal) |

## Strategic Partnerships

- **Anthropic:** Using SpaceX-linked AI compute capacity
- **xAI:** Elon Musk's own AI venture
- **Google:** In talks for orbital AI data centers
- **US Defense:** Via Starshield and Pentagon AI agreements

## Why SpaceX Matters for AI

1. **Orbital AI data centers solve ground constraints:** Land (no permit/zoning), power (continuous solar ~99%), cooling (radiative to deep space ~0K)
2. **Physical infrastructure expertise:** Hardware, manufacturing, satellites, power systems, global networking
3. **AI is becoming less software story and more capital/energy/hardware/infrastructure story**
4. **Companies without space access will depend on SpaceX or similar for orbital compute**
