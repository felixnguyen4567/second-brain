# Jay Alammar

Visual educator and author, one of the best technical explainers on the internet. Known for his illustrated transformer posts that demystify complex ML concepts with clear visuals.

## Works

- **Hands-On Large Language Models** (with Maarten Grootendorst, O'Reilly, Oct 2024) — Comprehensive visual guide to LLMs, builds vocabulary needed to understand everything else on the AI engineering stack.

## Notes

Jay's visual teaching style makes him the recommended starting point for engineers who want to build genuine mental models of how transformers and LLMs work before diving into production systems.
