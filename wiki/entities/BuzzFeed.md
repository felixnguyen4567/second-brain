# BuzzFeed

## Overview

**Digital media company** that went public via SPAC in 2021. In May 2026, **Byron Allen acquired a majority stake for $120M** — a "lifeboat" deal as the company faced severe financial difficulties. Shares rose nearly 200% on the news.

## Key Facts

- **Founded:** Digital media/publisher
- **Public:** Went public via SPAC in 2021
- **Crisis:** Severe financial difficulties leading to acquisition
- **Acquisition:** Byron Allen majority stake, $120M
- **Share price reaction:** +200% on announcement

## Why It Matters

- Symbol of digital media collapse post-SPAC boom
- Valuation collapse: SPAC valuation was vastly higher than $120M majority stake
- Byron Allen sees value where public markets do not
