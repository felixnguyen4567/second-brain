# Rush Shahani

Author of **Building Reliable AI Systems** (Manning). Focuses on the unsexy but critical part of AI engineering — reliability in production.

## Works

- **Building Reliable AI Systems** — Covers hallucination reduction, cost and performance optimization, bias and ethical output, monitoring. The book answers: "how do you not get paged at 2 AM because your LLM decided to make something up?"

## Notes

Reliability is what separates toys from products. This is the recommended reading for weeks 7-8 in the 2026 engineer's reading path.
