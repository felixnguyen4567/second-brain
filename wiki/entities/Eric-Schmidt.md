# Eric Schmidt

## Overview

**Eric Schmidt** — former CEO and Chairman of Google/Alphabet (2001–2011), now a venture capitalist and influential voice in AI. Publicly advised entrepreneurs to "found an agentic AI company," framing AI as a "compute-powered economy."

## Background

- **Tenure at Google:** Led Google from 2001–2011 as CEO, oversaw expansion to Android, YouTube, and Chrome
- **Chairman:** Alphabet 2011–2018
- **Post-Google:** Venture capital (Emergence Capital, et al.), advisor on AI policy

## 2026 Role in AI

- Publicly signaled that **autonomous AI agents** represent the next major investment wave
- Framed AI as part of a "compute-powered economy" — clearest Big Tech patriarch endorsement of agentic AI
- **Implication for Cognify Tech:** VC capital flooding agentic AI startups 2026–2027; early-stage opportunities remain but competitive moats are thin
