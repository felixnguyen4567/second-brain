# Sebastian Raschka, PhD

AI educator and researcher, known for his hands-on "from scratch" approach to teaching ML and LLM concepts. One of the best ML educators currently in the field according to peers.

## Works

- **Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)** (Manning) — Implement a GPT-like model in PyTorch from the ground up. Covers architecture, pre-training, the full pipeline.
- **Build a Reasoning Model (From Scratch)** (Manning) — Adds reasoning capabilities to a pre-trained LLM via RL, distillation, inference-time scaling.
- **Machine Learning Q and AI** (No Starch Press, 2024) — Q&A format covering deep learning concepts, multi-GPU training, transformer fine-tuning.
- **Machine Learning with PyTorch and Scikit-Learn** (Packt) — Comprehensive ML textbook.

## Notes

Sebastian's "Feynman approach": "What I cannot create, I do not understand." These books are for engineers who want to stop hand-waving about attention mechanisms and actually know what's happening. His books are consistently ranked as best-in-class for depth and hands-on learning.
