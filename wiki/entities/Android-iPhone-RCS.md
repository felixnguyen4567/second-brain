# Android-iPhone RCS Encryption

## Overview

**End-to-end encryption for messages between Android and iPhone devices** — finally achieved in May 2026 through Apple's adoption of **RCS (Rich Communication Services)**. This ends the era of insecure "green bubble" messaging and represents one of the biggest changes in mobile messaging history.

## Key Facts

- **What changed:** Apple adopted RCS, enabling E2E encryption between Android and iPhone
- **What ended:** Insecure cross-platform messaging; "green bubble" insecurity
- **Years in making:** Google pushed Apple for years to adopt RCS
- **Impact:** All smartphone users globally affected

## Why It Matters

1. **Security:** E2E encryption now standard across all major smartphone platforms
2. **End of green bubble insecurity:** iPhone-Android SMS gaps in security resolved
3. **Google's multi-year campaign succeeded:** Apple finally capitulated on RCS
4. **One of biggest messaging changes in history:** Standards convergence
