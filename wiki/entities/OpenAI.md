# OpenAI

## Overview
Leading AI research lab and creator of the GPT and o-series model families.

## Products & Models
- **GPT series**: GPT-4, GPT-4o, GPT-5 (consumer/enterprise)
- **o-series**: o1, o3, o3-pro (reasoning-focused)
- **ChatGPT**: Consumer product ($20/month Plus, $200/month Pro)
