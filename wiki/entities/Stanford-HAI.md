# Stanford HAI (Human-Centered AI Institute)

Stanford University's Institute for Human-Centered AI. Publishes annual **AI Index Report** — the most comprehensive tracking of AI progress and societal preparedness.

## 2026 AI Index Report — Key Findings

- **AI not plateauing:** Capability accelerating and reaching more people than ever
- **Industry dominance:** Industry produced 90%+ of notable frontier models in 2025
- **PhD-level performance:** AI achieves PhD-level on multiple benchmarks
- **SWE-bench:** AI went from 60% → ~100% accuracy in one year (coding tasks)
- **Cybersecurity agents:** 15% → 93% accuracy in same period
- **Productivity:** AI boosts customer support by 14-15%, coding by 26%
- **Preparedness gap:** "Gap between what AI can do and how prepared society is to manage it is growing faster than ever"
- **US-China race:** Intensifying AI competition covered

## Significance

The 2026 report is a validation of the AI engineering space — capability is real and accelerating. The preparedness gap is the key risk/opportunity for enterprise AI consultancies like Cognify Tech.
