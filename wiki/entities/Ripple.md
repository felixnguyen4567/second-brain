# Ripple

## Overview

**Cryptocurrency and fintech company** behind XRP and the RippleNet payment network. In May 2026, secured a **$200M credit facility from Neuberger Berman** to expand Ripple Prime — institutional prime brokerage for trading both crypto and traditional markets.

## Key Facts

- **Product:** XRP cryptocurrency, RippleNet payment network
- **New funding:** $200M credit facility from Neuberger Berman
- **Purpose:** Expand Ripple Prime (institutional prime brokerage)
- **Scope:** Both crypto and traditional market trading

## Why It Matters

- Ripple actively building institutional financial infrastructure bridging crypto and traditional finance
- $200M credit facility shows traditional finance institutions (Neuberger Berman) backing crypto infrastructure
- Prime brokerage expansion signals growing institutional adoption of crypto
