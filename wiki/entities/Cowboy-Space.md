# Cowboy Space

## Overview

Startup pursuing **orbital AI data centers** (also known as Aetherflux). Raised **$275M** in May 2026 to build data centers in space — but faces core problem: **not enough rockets to launch** and costs still too high for widespread viability.

## Key Facts

- **Focus:** Orbital AI data centers / space compute
- **Funding:** $275M raised
- **Core challenge:** Insufficient launch capacity; high costs

## Why It Matters

- Represents the orbital compute trend — AI companies looking to space for compute
- Cowboy Space's struggle with rocket availability highlights SpaceX's strategic advantage as the only company with sufficient launch capacity
- Signal that orbital AI data centers are technically feasible but economically challenging
