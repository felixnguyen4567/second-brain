# Chip Huyen

ML engineer, author, and Stanford instructor. Writes consistently engineering-first, not research-first — making her books practical for practitioners who ship.

## Works

- **AI Engineering: Building Applications with Foundation Models** (O'Reilly, Jan 2025) — LLM-era version of her classic. Covers how real applications get built with foundation models: data, RAG, evaluations, deployment tradeoffs, failure modes.
- **Designing Machine Learning Systems** (O'Reilly) — Classic on ML systems at scale, production readiness.

## Notes

Chip's framing is consistently engineering-first, which makes her books practical in a way most books in this category miss. She focuses on the systems view that most tutorials skip entirely — especially the "when to fine-tune vs. when to use foundation models" decision.
