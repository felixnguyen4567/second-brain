# Agentic AI

LLM-based systems that autonomously automate complex tasks by planning, using tools, and iterating on results. One of the hottest areas in AI (2026).

## 2026 News Highlights

### Pentagon AI Deal (Virality: 9/10)
US Department of Defense signed agreements with OpenAI, Google, Nvidia, Microsoft, AWS, and SpaceX for classified military AI projects. Marks major military entry into AI era.

### China AI Worker Lawsuit (Virality: 8/10)
Hangzhou court ruled in favor of a tech worker replaced by AI — first legal precedent for worker rights vs automation wave.

### US Agency Guidance on Agentic AI
Multiple US government agencies issued guidance on adopting agentic AI systems, highlighting security challenges and risks.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Autonomous Task Completion** — Plans and executes multi-step workflows
2. **Tool Use** — Calls external APIs, databases, file systems
3. **Iterative Refinement** — Self-corrects based on feedback
4. **Memory Persistence** — Learns from past interactions
