# AI Startup Moat

## Core Problem

Eric Schmidt's advice to "found an agentic AI company" is correct about opportunity but incomplete about durability. **The moat question:** Anyone can call an LLM API. If your "agentic" product is just a better prompt wrapper, you'll be undercut when base models ship native capability.

## The Reliability Compound Error Problem

- 95% accurate single-step AI = useful
- 95% accurate 10-step agent = **~60% chance** of completing the workflow correctly
- Compound errors across multiple steps = agentic systems can fail catastrophically
- **Reliability is the product**

## What Creates Real Moat

1. **Deeply specific to industry or geography** — training data and relationships create defensibility an API call can't replicate
2. **Proprietary workflow knowledge** — encoded know-how specific to a niche
3. **Trust and relationships** — local SME customers who need accountability
4. **Reliable execution at scale** — the hard part is not the AI, it's the reliability layer

## For Cognify Tech

- **Digital premium accounts** = market entry (creates relationships)
- **Real leverage** = agentic workflows for Darwin SMEs: appointment scheduling, customer follow-ups, inventory management
- **Target customer:** Business that needs 24/7 AI employee, can't afford enterprise AI consulting, pays $200/month
- **Moat:** Specific to Darwin geography and SME workflows = defensible against base model commoditization
