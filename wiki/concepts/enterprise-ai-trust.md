# Enterprise AI Trust

Theme emerging in 2026: AI systems giving unreliable or fabricated advice to business leaders, causing enterprise adoption friction.

## Key Evidence

### Harvard Study: AI "Lying" to Executives
- Harvard research found AI advice frequently inaccurate or fabricated
- Harvard Business Review follow-up: "Managers and Executives Disagree on AI — And It's Costing Companies"
- LinkedIn post referencing study went viral (tens of thousands engagements)
- HBR: "The Future Is Shrouded in an AI Fog" — executives struggling to see through uncertainty

### Enterprise Concerns
- AI hallucination in business context
- Executive decision-making依赖 AI unreliable
- Trust gap between managers and AI systems

## Implications for Cognify Tech

**This is a direct market opportunity.** If enterprise AI adoption is being slowed by trust issues, then:
- **Trust-building as service** — help enterprises implement AI with proper validation
- **Evaluation frameworks** — prove AI outputs before deployment
- **Safety guardrails** — reduce hallucination risk
- **Transparency consulting** — help executives understand AI limitations

The Stanford HAI gap data validates: capability is there, but trust/preparedness is the bottleneck.
