# War Powers Resolution

## Overview

The **War Powers Resolution** (1973) is a U.S. federal law intended to check the President's power to commit the US to armed conflict without congressional approval. Requires the President to notify Congress within 48 hours of committing armed forces to hostilities, and prohibits armed forces from remaining for more than 60 days without congressional authorization.
