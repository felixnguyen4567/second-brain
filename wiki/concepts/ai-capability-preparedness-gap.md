# AI Capability-Preparedness Gap

Key theme from Stanford HAI 2026 AI Index: **AI capability is accelerating faster than societal/institutional preparedness to manage it.**

## Key Data Points

- AI achieves PhD-level performance on multiple benchmarks
- SWE-bench: 60% → 100% accuracy in one year (coding agents)
- Cybersecurity agents: 15% → 93% accuracy in same period
- Enterprise AI adoption is real but trust issues emerging
- Governance frameworks lagging behind deployment speed

## Why It Matters

The widening gap creates both **risk** and **opportunity**:

### Risk
- AI incidents (database deletion, agent loops, hallucination) growing
- Regulation catching up to deployment
- Enterprise trust issues (Harvard AI "lying" study)

### Opportunity
- **Cognify Tech positioning:** Help enterprises navigate the gap
- Consulting: AI implementation with proper guardrails
- Evaluation and safety frameworks
- Trust-building as a service

## Key Quotes

> "While AI continues its remarkable advancement, our institutional preparedness is failing to keep pace." — Stanford HAI 2026 AI Index
