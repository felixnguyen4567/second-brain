# AI Compute Leverage

## Definition

**AI compute leverage** refers to the strategic advantage gained through control of the physical infrastructure that runs AI models — GPUs, electricity, data centers, networking, cooling, uptime, and inference capacity. The concept captures how compute is not just an operational cost but a **competitive constraint and leverage point**.

## Why "Compute Is The New Oil"

"Compute is the new oil" because:

1. **Scarce:** High-end GPUs (H100, GB200) remain supply-constrained despite massive investment
2. **Capital-intensive:** Building and operating AI infrastructure requires billions in upfront capital
3. **Supply-constrained:** Chip production limited by TSMC and a few manufacturers
4. **Strategically sensitive:** Compute access determines who can train frontier models, serve millions of users, price aggressively, and experiment faster

## How Compute Shapes Product

Compute affects every dimension of AI product viability:

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| **Latency** | How fast AI responds to users |
| **Context capacity** | How much information can be processed |
| **User volume** | How many concurrent users supported |
| **Pricing** | What price business can charge |
| **Profitability** | Whether advanced features are profitable |
| **Reliability** | Whether system improves or collapses under demand |

## The Infrastructure Sets The Growth Ceiling

> "Hạ tầng quyết định trần tăng trưởng" — Infrastructure sets the growth ceiling.

A product can have a beautiful demo and strong brand, but if the underlying system is too expensive, too slow, or too dependent on someone else's capacity — the product hits a wall.

## The Founder Trap

Mistaking **model capability** for **company defensibility**. If everyone can access similar models via API, the moat must come from:
- Workflow data
- Distribution
- Trust
- Integrations
- Compliance
- Latency
- Reliability
- Cost structure
- Domain expertise
