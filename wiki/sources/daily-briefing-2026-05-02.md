## Top Stories

### 1. Trump Declares Iran Hostilities "Terminated"
The Trump administration sent a letter to Congress declaring that hostilities with Iran have "terminated," claiming no congressional approval is needed. The move comes on the 60-day War Powers Resolution deadline. Trump cited a ceasefire since April 7. Senate Republicans blocked the Iran War Powers Resolution for the 6th time. A constitutional showdown is brewing.

### 2. Pentagon Signs 7 AI Companies for Classified Military Work
Defense Department announced deals with Google, Microsoft, AWS, Nvidia, OpenAI, SpaceX, and Reflection AI to deploy AI on classified networks. Notably, Anthropic was excluded. These will "augment warfighter decision-making."

### 3. DeepSeek V4 Launches with 1M Context Window
DeepSeek dropped the V4 preview (April 24), open-sourcing a model reportedly outperforming GPT-5.2 and Claude on benchmarks. Built for Huawei chips — a strategic move for China's AI independence. 1M token context window, millisecond latency, strong agentic reasoning.

### 4. S&P 500 & NASDAQ Hit Fresh Record Highs
Friday May 1 close: Dow Jones +1.62% (49,652), S&P 500 +1.02% (7,209), Nasdaq +0.9%. AI trade fueling the rally. S&P 500 logged its biggest monthly gain since November 2020.

### 5. Spirit Airlines Shutting Down
Spirit Airlines confirmed it's going out of business after 34 years. Government bailout talks with the Trump administration collapsed. No deal for $500M cash infusion. All flights halted.

### 6. Eric Schmidt: "Found an Agentic AI Company"
The former Google CEO publicly advised entrepreneurs to start agentic AI companies, framing AI as part of a "compute-powered economy." Clearest signal yet from Big Tech patriarch that autonomous AI agents represent the next gold rush.

### 7. Meta Threatens to Pull Apps from New Mexico
Trial Phase 2 begins May 4. A jury already awarded NM $375M against Meta for harming children via addictive algorithm design. Meta threatening to shut down Facebook/Instagram in New Mexico if forced to implement safety reforms.

### 8. IDC Warns: Smartphone Market to Crash 13% on Chip Crunch
Market research firm IDC forecasts global smartphone shipments to fall 13% due to memory chip shortage. Geopolitical supply chain disruptions cited.

### 9. US Pulling 5,000 Troops from Germany
Pentagon ordered withdrawal after Chancellor Friedrich Merz suggested Iranian negotiators had influenced US-German relations. ~35,000 US troops remain in Germany.

### 10. Q1 Earnings Season Summary
S&P 500 earnings expected +14% YoY for Q1 2026. Meta slipping on AI capex costs. Alphabet, Apple, Amazon, Microsoft results in focus next week.
