# "Quiet After The Storm — One Year of India-Pakistan Ceasefire" Journal

## Summary

Personal reflection on the one-year anniversary of the India-Pakistan conflict in May 2025. While the ceasefire holds, it is fragile — Pakistan's military has warned of "stronger responses" to future provocations, and analysts say the next conflict will escalate faster and be more dangerous. Kashmir, water, and disputed territory remain unresolved. The author reflects on heritage, identity as someone of South Asian background living in Australia, and the gap between decision-makers and those who live in the blast radius.

## Key Points

1. **Conflict timeline:** 4 days of intense conflict in May 2025 brought two nuclear-armed nations closer to direct combat than any point since 1971
2. **Ceasefire fragility:** Pakistan's military explicitly warned of "stronger responses" to future provocations
3. **Next conflict risk:** Analysts at Council on Foreign Relations warn next conflict will escalate faster and be more dangerous
4. **Unresolved tensions:** Kashmir, water, disputed territory, historical grievance — not resolved, only deferred
5. **Deferral ≠ Resolution:** "The silence after the storm is not peace. It is the space where the next storm is being prepared."
6. **Personal dimension:** Author lives in Australia; South Asian heritage; identity question "which side are you on?" — neither, both, "this is not a sports match"
7. **Connection to professional life:** Gap between what a system promises and what it delivers is where people get hurt
