# "The Real AI Moat Might Be The Boring Stuff" Journal

## Summary

Reflection on the Anthropic-SpaceX compute story. Argues that while the AI world obsesses over the visible layer (models, demos, prompts, agents, benchmarks), the real power is accumulating in the boring layer: GPUs, electricity, data centers, networking, uptime, margins, and distribution. Infrastructure sets the growth ceiling.

## Key Insights

1. **The Demo Layer Is Seductive:** A demo can work once. A business has to work every day, under load, with real users, real costs, real errors, real privacy constraints, and real expectations.
2. **"Compute is a product constraint":** Affects latency, context capacity, user volume, pricing, profitability, reliability under demand.
3. **In Vietnamese:** "Hạ tầng quyết định trần tăng trưởng" — Infrastructure sets the growth ceiling.
4. **Founder trap:** Mistaking model capability for company defensibility. If everyone can access similar models, the moat must come from elsewhere (workflow data, distribution, trust, integrations, compliance, latency, reliability, cost structure, domain expertise).
5. **Quiet foundations that matter:** Clear boundaries, reliable memory, good logs, human approval points, cost control, recovery from failure, simple interfaces, repeatable workflows.
6. **"Do not only build for the demo. Build for the operating rhythm after the demo."**
