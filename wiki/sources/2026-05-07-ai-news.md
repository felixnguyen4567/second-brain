## Core Story

Reuters Breakingviews reported that Anthropic uses AI compute capacity connected to SpaceX. This is not just a partnership headline — it is a signal that the center of gravity in AI is moving deeper into infrastructure.

## Compute Is Becoming The New Oil

"Compute is the new oil" is an overused phrase but keeps becoming more accurate. AI compute is scarce, capital-intensive, supply-constrained, and strategically sensitive. It determines:
- Who can train frontier models
- Who can serve millions of users
- Who can price products aggressively
- Who can experiment faster than competitors

## Why SpaceX Changes the Conversation

SpaceX has deep experience with hardware, manufacturing, launch cadence, satellites, power systems, networking, and operating critical infrastructure. Starlink provides a global communications layer most AI companies lack.

If AI demand keeps rising, the bottleneck will not be model architecture. It will be where to put the GPUs, how to power them, how to connect them, and how to keep costs low enough for products to scale.

## The New AI Power Stack

If orbital AI data centers become real, the competitive landscape shifts in ways that favor:
- **Companies with space access** — SpaceX, Blue Origin, China
- **Companies with energy efficiency advantages** — radiative cooling is free in space
- **Companies with distribution** — Starlink-to-orbital-data-center pathway

It disadvantages:
- **Traditional data center operators** — CBRE, Equinix, Digital Realty
- **Nations without space launch capability**

## The Risk: Infrastructure Dependence

If frontier AI labs depend heavily on external compute providers, they inherit strategic dependencies. A model company may own the weights, product experience, and brand — but if it cannot secure compute, it cannot compete at the frontier.

## Lessons for Builders

For founders and operators:
- Understand which layer of the AI stack you are really building on
- If your product depends on a frontier model API, your margins and roadmap are partly controlled by someone else
- AI product strategy now requires infrastructure literacy

The real question: can the system run reliably, affordably, and safely when usage grows by 10x?
