# "The Most Dangerous AI Feature Is The One You Promised Too Early" Journal

## Summary

Reflection on Apple paying $250M for delayed Siri AI features. Core argument: AI promises are becoming liabilities when they outrun delivery. The gap between what customers believed they were getting and what the product actually delivered is where trust dies.

## Key Insights

1. **The Demo Is Not The Product:** A good demo proves possibility; a good product proves reliability.
2. **"Trust is a product feature"** — if users cannot tell what AI can do, cannot do, and when to verify output, they are not using a product, they are gambling with a black box.
3. **Apple's challenge:** Phone assistant close to real life (messages, calendars, photos, payments, locations, family routines) = higher duty of care.
4. **For builders — precise promises:**
   - This is what the AI can do now
   - This is what still requires review
   - This is what it will never do without permission
   - This is where the logs are
   - This is how you recover from mistakes
5. **"In AI, never let your marketing outrun your operating system. Ship the capability. Show the limitation. Earn the trust. Then expand."**
