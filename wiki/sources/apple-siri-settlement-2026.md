# Apple $250M Siri Settlement

## Summary

Apple agreed to pay $250 million to settle claims from iPhone owners over AI Siri features that were advertised but not delivered on schedule. This signals the end of cheap "AI promise" marketing — when customers buy based on AI claims, those claims become legal and financial obligations.

## Key Points

- **$250M settlement** for delayed AI Siri features
- **Apple's challenge:** On-device vs. cloud inference, privacy, latency, multilingual scale, app integration, safety across deeply personal data
- **Siri competes not against old voice assistants but against ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and agentic tools**
- **Apple brand built on "just works" reliability — AI does not naturally behave that way**

## Key Insights

1. **AI vaporware is becoming expensive:** The window for attaching "AI" to roadmap and enjoying upside immediately is closing
2. **Trust is a product feature:** Users need to know what AI can do, what it cannot do, and when to verify output
3. **The demo-product gap:** A demo proves possibility; a product proves reliability
4. **Apple's special risk:** Brand built on trust + deeply personal data access (photos, messages, health, payments) = higher duty of care
5. **Builder checklist:** Separate shipped from roadmap; make limitations visible; define human approval requirements; keep logs and recovery paths
