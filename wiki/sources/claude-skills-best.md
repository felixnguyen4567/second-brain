# I Tried 100 Claude Skills. These Are The Best

## Summary

The PyCoach tested 100 Claude Skills and ranked the best 11 across 4 categories. Skills let Claude follow reusable instructions — brand colors, formatting, workflows — making outputs consistent and brand-aligned.

**Top 11 Skills:**
1. **Slide Deck Builder** — turns rough idea into full slide sequence with visual structure
2. **Explainer Graphic** — converts concepts into HTML explainers with visual analogies
3. **Branded Carousel Generator** — creates brand-styled social carousels
4. **Brand Applicator** — applies brand rules (fonts, colors, logos) to any document
5. **Market Competitors** — generates competitive intelligence reports from websites
6. **Workflow Visualizer** — turns processes into interactive visual maps
7. **Contract Reviewer** — breaks down contracts: key terms, red flags, missing protections
8. **Decision Council** — runs multiple expert personas for complex decisions
9. **Quick Research** — compresses research into structured briefs
10. **Frontend Design** — official Claude Code skill for production-ready UI
11. **Implement Design** — Figma-to-code translation with tight visual fidelity

## Key Insight

Skills bridge the gap between generic AI output and brand-consistent, production-ready deliverables. For knowledge workers and marketers, the top skills save hours per week on repetitive formatting, research, and design work.
