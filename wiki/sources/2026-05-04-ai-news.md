## Key Points

### OpenAI Misses Q1/2026 Revenue Target
For the first time in years, OpenAI reported revenue below internal expectations for Q1/2026. Anthropic showed impressive enterprise growth, narrowing the gap significantly. Google is investing heavily in AI agents for business.

### Three Major Model Releases in May 2026
The month of May 2026 saw three major releases:
- **DeepSeek V4** (open-source, outperforms GPT-5.2 and Claude on benchmarks)
- **GPT-5.5-Cyber** (OpenAI, cybersecurity-focused)
- **Claude Opus 4.7** (Anthropic)

### GPT-5.5-Cyber Launch
After being overtaken by Anthropic on revenue and valuation, Sam Altman decided to release GPT-5.5-Cyber — an AI model designed specifically for cybersecurity, immediately deployed to "essential cyberspace defense units."

### AI-as-a-Service Replacing Traditional SaaS
Both Anthropic and OpenAI announced joint ventures with asset management companies. The AI market is shifting from traditional SaaS models to AI-as-a-Service.

### $3.5 Billion Wafer-Scale Chip Startup IPO
Despite overall tech slowdown, a startup developing wafer-scale chips IPOed at $3.5 billion, showing continued strong capital flow into semiconductors.

## Market Implications
- AI-as-a-Service is replacing traditional SaaS
- Competition is no longer just about model capability but ecosystem
- Google is aggressively freeing AI agents to catch up with Anthropic
- Startup chip companies still attracting massive investment despite broader tech slowdown
