# "Ba Cuộc Đua, Một Bài Học" Journal

## Summary

Personal reflection on OpenAI missing Q1/2026 revenue targets while Anthropic and Google close the gap. Core thesis: OpenAI did not lose because their product is worse — they lost because they were running a race the market has already changed. The market shifted from AI chatbots to AI agents, and Anthropic understood this early.

## Key Insights

1. **Three parallel races:**
   - OpenAI vs Anthropic — model AI race (benchmark ≠ revenue)
   - DeepSeek V4, GPT-5.5, Claude Opus 4.7 — new models launching monthly
   - AI-as-a-Service replacing SaaS — joint ventures with asset management firms

2. **The real lesson:** "Nobody wins a race by chasing competitors. In an ecosystem, the winner isn't the fastest — it's the fastest to adapt."

3. **For builders:** Don't need to be OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google. Just need to understand a narrow domain deeply enough to see what others miss, build something good enough others want to use it, and keep moving when others stop.

4. **Market is an ecosystem, not a single race:** Different companies chose different races and are winning their own races.
