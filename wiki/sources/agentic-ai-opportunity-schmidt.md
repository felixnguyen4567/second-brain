# Why Eric Schmidt Is Right (And Why It Should Make You Nervous)

## Summary

Personal reflection on Eric Schmidt's advice to "found an agentic AI company if you want to make money." Analysis covers why Schmidt is correct about the economic opportunity, where his advice is strategically incomplete, and what this means for Cognify Tech.

**Key insight from journal:** The real agentic AI opportunity for Cognify Tech is not in general AI subscriptions — it's in specific, reliable AI automation for Darwin-based SMEs who need 24/7 AI employees at $200/month.

## Eric Schmidt's Argument

- Agentic AI creates new work that didn't exist before (not just cost savings)
- Tooling layer for agentic AI is still immature = highest-value applications not yet built
- Gap: large enterprises too slow, individual developers building toys
- **Cognify Tech opportunity:** specific workflows for specific businesses where off-the-shelf tools don't exist

## The Reliability Problem

- 95% accurate single-step AI = useful
- 95% accurate 10-step agent = ~60% chance of completing the workflow correctly
- **Reliability is the product**
- Moat question: prompt wrappers will be undercut when base models ship native capability

## The Warning in Schmidt's Optimism

"Make money" and "build something that lasts" are different problems. Real moat = deeply specific to particular industries or geographies, where training data and relationships create genuine defensibility.

## For Cognify Tech

- Digital premium accounts (ChatGPT Plus, ELSA, Netflix) = market entry, creates relationships
- Real leverage = agentic workflows for small businesses that can't afford enterprise AI consulting
- **Specific Darwin opportunity:** appointment scheduling, customer follow-ups, inventory management
- AI employee that works 24/7, costs $200/month
