# OpenAI Launches o3-pro

## Summary
OpenAI released o3-pro, their most advanced reasoning model. Key highlights:
- Extended thinking time (up to 10 min per problem)
- 92% accuracy on GPQA Diamond benchmark
- $200/month (ChatGPT Pro) or $20/$80 per 1M tokens (API)
- Targets complex math, science, and multi-step reasoning

## Key Takeaways
1. **Reasoning models are the new battleground** — OpenAI, [[Anthropic]], and [[Google DeepMind]] all competing here
2. **Cost vs capability tradeoff** — premium pricing limits mass adoption
3. **Speed sacrifice** — 30-60s response time, not suitable for real-time use

## Connections
- Competes with [[Claude 3.5 Opus]] and [[Gemini 2.5 Pro]]
- Part of [[OpenAI]]'s o-series evolution: o1 → o3 → o3-pro
